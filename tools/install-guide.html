One last step

My Facebook is downloaded. Your browser only lets you switch an extension on, so finish here. It takes about a minute.

  1. Open your browser's Extensions page

    Copy this address, open a new tab (Ctrl + T), paste it into the address bar and press Enter.

    chrome://extensions

    Websites cannot open that page for you, which is why it is a copy and paste.

  2. Switch on Developer mode

    It is a switch in the top-right corner of the Extensions page. It only allows loading an extension from a folder.

  3. Click "Load unpacked" and pick the folder

    Copy the folder below. In the window that opens, click the address bar at the top, paste (Ctrl + V), press Enter, then click Select Folder.

    (the folder shown in the installer window)

    Leave that folder where it is. The browser loads the extension from it every time it starts.

  4. Open Facebook

    Go to facebook.com. Your home page now shows friends and accounts you follow. Click the My Facebook icon in the toolbar for its settings (pin it from the puzzle-piece menu if you do not see it).

Why the extra clicks? Chromium browsers (Brave, Chrome, Edge, Vivaldi, Opera) block every program from adding an extension that does not come from their store. That protects you from software installing extensions behind your back, and it means no installer can do this part.
